I received my Ph.D. in 2024 from Newcastle University, under the supervision of Dr. [Tom Nye](https://tomnye.github.io/) . My [doctoral work](https://theses.ncl.ac.uk/jspui/handle/10443/6588) focused on statistical modelling on Riemannian manifold, which now forms the mathematical foundation of my research in bioinformatics. Geometrical ideas often influence the way I think about and approach complex problems.

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow (Principal Investigator) at Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMS), ShanghaiTech University, working with Prof. [Pengcheng Zeng](https://pengchengzeng.github.io/)（曾鹏程）. My research lies at the intersection of non-Euclidean statistics and spatial omics.
