I received my Ph.D. in 2024 from Newcastle University, under the supervision of Dr. Tom Nye ([Thesis](https://theses.ncl.ac.uk/jspui/handle/10443/6588)). My doctoral work focused on statistical modeling on non-Euclidean spaces, which now forms the mathematical foundation of my research in bioinformatics. Geometrical ideas often influence the way I think about and approach complex problems.

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow (Principal Investigator) at ShanghaiTech University, working with Prof. [Pengcheng Zeng](https://pengchengzeng.github.io/). My research lies at the intersection of non-Euclidean statistics and spatial omics.

### Research Interests
Non-Euclidean Statistics, Deep Learning, Bioinformatics
